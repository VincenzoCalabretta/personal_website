## What just happened

1. Bazel resolved `//tools/zola` to the pre-built Zola binary fetched via `http_archive`.
2. The `zola_site` rule ran `zola build` inside a sandboxed action.
3. The output landed in `bazel-bin/site/site_public/`.

## Next steps
